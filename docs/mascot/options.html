Miblo mascot: Sphynx cat options

Three directions to replace the placeholder coral blob. Every shape below is drawn through a tiny JS mirror of ui::Canvas (fillRect, fillRoundRect, fillCircle, fillTriangle, wideLine, arc, text), with no paths, curves or gradients, so whichever option wins ports 1:1 to screens::mascot(). Coordinates are integer offsets × u from the mascot centre, inside the same ~96 px cleared region the boot screen already uses. The big screens animate through the loading frames.

New palette entries (RGB565 for ui::color)

Porting notes

  • arc() uses the TFT_eSPI convention: 0° = 6 o'clock, clockwise; spans that wrap past 0° (mouths, collar) are drawn as a0 > a1.
  • wideLine() has round caps: ears are 3 fanned lines each; almond eyes are one slanted capsule.
  • The 48 px version is the same function at u = 0.5 with a small flag that skips wrinkles and highlights.
  • Distinct from Anthropic/Claude branding: pink/peach skin, no orange, no starburst or asterisk, and a clear cat silhouette with ears and eyes.
  • Primitive counts include the one background fillRect that clears the region each frame.
